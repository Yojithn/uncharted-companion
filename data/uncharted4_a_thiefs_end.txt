Uncharted 4: A Thief's End (2016) is set several years after the previous games, with Nathan Drake retired from treasure hunting and married to Elena Fisher, working a mundane salvage job. His life is upended when his older brother, Samuel Drake, believed dead for over a decade, reappears. Sam explains he faked his fate after a failed prison escape in Panama and has spent the years since working off a debt to crime boss Hector Alcazar, who now demands Sam find the lost treasure of the 17th-century pirate Henry Avery or be killed.

Nathan is drawn back into the world he promised to leave behind, and the brothers set off in search of Libertalia, a legendary pirate colony in Madagascar founded on the principle of a share-and-share-alike society among former slaves, mutineers, and escaped criminals, believed to house Avery's treasure. They are aided by Victor Sullivan and opposed by Rafe Adler, a wealthy rival treasure hunter who originally funded Sam's search, and Nadine Ross, leader of the private military company Shoreline, whom Rafe has hired.

The trail leads through Scotland, Madagascar, and eventually to a fortified pirate colony in the jungle, where the group discovers Libertalia was destroyed generations earlier in a civil war between its founders, Henry Avery and fellow pirate captain Thomas Tew, over the treasure itself. Rafe's obsession with the treasure and his rivalry with the Drake brothers culminates in a fight amid the fortress, which collapses during a storm; Rafe dies in the wreckage. The game ends years later showing Nathan and Elena's daughter, Cassie, being introduced to the same sense of adventure that defined her father's life, marking the narrative conclusion of Nathan Drake's story.
