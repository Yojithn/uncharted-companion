Nathan Drake is a modern-day treasure hunter and the protagonist of Uncharted 4: A Thief's End. He claims descent from the 16th-century English explorer Sir Francis Drake, though this lineage is uncertain. Nathan was raised in orphanages and group homes after his mother's death and first met his mentor Victor Sullivan as a teenager in Cartagena, Colombia. By the start of Uncharted 4, Nathan has retired from treasure hunting and works a mundane salvage-diving job, married to Elena Fisher, until his brother Sam's reappearance draws him back into the world of treasure hunting one last time.

Victor "Sully" Sullivan is Nathan Drake's mentor, best friend, and father figure, a veteran treasure hunter and con man with a taste for cigars and expensive tastes he can rarely afford. In Uncharted 4 he joins Nathan and Sam on the search for Henry Avery's treasure, providing experience, contacts, and comic relief while also voicing caution about the risks the brothers are taking.

Elena Fisher is an investigative journalist and Nathan Drake's wife. In Uncharted 4 their marriage is under strain: Nathan has been keeping his salvage-diving side jobs secret from her, and when Sam's reappearance pulls Nathan back into treasure hunting, Elena initially believes he has broken his promise to leave that life behind. She later joins him in Madagascar once she learns the truth, and the game explores how the couple reconciles the thrill of adventure with a stable married life.

Samuel "Sam" Drake is Nathan's older brother, believed dead for over a decade after a failed prison escape attempt in Panama. In truth, Sam faked his death and spent fifteen years working off a debt to crime boss Hector Alcazar. Sam resurfaces at the start of Uncharted 4 needing Nathan's help to find Henry Avery's treasure or be killed by Alcazar's men, setting the entire plot in motion. Sam is more impulsive and morally flexible than Nathan, and the tension between the brothers over trust and honesty is a central thread of the story.

Nadine Ross is the mercenary leader of the private military company Shoreline, serving as an antagonist in Uncharted 4 alongside Rafe Adler. She is a disciplined, no-nonsense South African soldier for hire, hired by Rafe to provide the manpower needed to beat the Drake brothers to Libertalia.

Rafe Adler is the primary antagonist of Uncharted 4, a wealthy, privileged treasure hunter obsessed with proving himself by finding Henry Avery's lost pirate treasure. Rafe originally funded Sam Drake's search for Avery's treasure and later partners with Nadine Ross's Shoreline mercenaries after Sam escapes his control. Driven by insecurity and a need for validation rather than money alone, Rafe becomes increasingly ruthless and unstable as the hunt for Libertalia progresses, ultimately clashing fatally with Nathan and Sam in the collapsing pirate fortress.

Hector Alcazar is a crime boss mentioned throughout the early part of Uncharted 4; Sam owes him a debt from their years working together after Sam's presumed death, and it is this debt that forces Sam to return and pull Nathan into the search for Avery's treasure.
