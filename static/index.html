How it answers

Nothing is made up. Everything is looked up.

  1. 01

    Search

    Your question is matched against the walkthrough notes by meaning, not by exact words.

  2. 02

    Re-rank

    The twenty closest notes are read side by side with your question, and only the best few are kept.

  3. 03

    Answer

    An AI model writes the reply from those notes alone, and if one provider is busy, the next takes over. If the notes don't cover it, you'll be told, not given a guess.
